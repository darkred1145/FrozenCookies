Cookie Clicker mod

FrozenCookies

It plays Cookie Clicker for you: picks the best buys, clicks the golden stuff, casts the spells, and minds the bank. You just watch the numbers go up.

Install now View source

What it does

Efficient autobuy

Every building and upgrade gets ranked by what it actually earns you. The best deal gets bought, on its own.

Real bulk buying

Set x10 or x100 and it buys the biggest chunk you can afford while keeping your bank intact.

Golden cookies and timers

Clicks golden cookies, reindeer, and fortunes the moment they show up. Spawn timers stay on screen even when one is overdue.

Spells and combos

Casts grimoire spells for you, chains double FTHOF combos, and never buys past your mana cap.

Pantheon and dragon

Slots your gods, keeps Cyclius rotating, upgrades and pets the dragon, sets your auras.

Banks and seasons

Holds lucky, harvest, and manual banks. Farms seasons in your fixed order or by best value.

Install

Web console

  1. Open Cookie Clicker.
  2. Hit F12 and open the Console tab.
  3. Paste the line below and hit enter.
Game.LoadMod("https://darkred1145.github.io/FrozenCookies/frozen_cookies.js");

Steam Workshop

  1. Subscribe to Frozen Cookies.
  2. Restart Cookie Clicker.
  3. Flip it on under Options, Manage mods.

No Workshop? Copy the Steam/FrozenCookies folder into mods/local instead.

Tampermonkey

  1. Make a userscript for the game URL.
  2. Point it at the mod file below.
https://darkred1145.github.io/FrozenCookies/frozen_cookies.js
