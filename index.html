Automate Cookie Clicker: efficient autobuy with real bulk buying, golden cookies, spells, pantheon, seasons, and banks — all computed for you.

Install now View source

What it does

Efficient autobuy

Ranks buildings and upgrades by CpS efficiency and buys the best next purchase automatically.

Real bulk buying

Buys the largest affordable count up to 10/100 while keeping bank reserves — not one by one.

Golden cookies & timers

Auto-clicks golden cookies, reindeer, and fortunes. Spawn timers never vanish, even when overdue.

Spells & combos

Auto-casts grimoire spells, double-cast FTHOF combos, and respects your wizard tower mana cap.

Pantheon & dragon

Auto-slots gods, swaps Cyclius, upgrades and pets the dragon, sets auras.

Banks & seasons

Lucky, harvest, and manual banks. Season farming in fixed order or by efficiency.

Install

Web (console)

  1. Open Cookie Clicker.
  2. Press F12, go to Console.
  3. Paste and run:
Game.LoadMod("https://darkred1145.github.io/FrozenCookies/frozen_cookies.js");

Steam Workshop

  1. Subscribe to Frozen Cookies.
  2. Restart Cookie Clicker.
  3. Enable it under Options → Manage mods.

Steam manual install: copy the Steam/FrozenCookies folder into mods/local.

Tampermonkey

  1. Create a userscript for the game URL.
  2. Load the mod URL below on Game.ready.
https://darkred1145.github.io/FrozenCookies/frozen_cookies.js
